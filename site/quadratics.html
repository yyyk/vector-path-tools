toQuadratics comparison

The same cubic, replaced by quadratics by each of toQuadratics's methods (the library's own code), all at the same tolerance. Drag the round handles. Gray band: the cubic, drawn as wide as twice the tolerance, so the quadratics should stay inside it. The quadratics alternate in color and join at the dots. The marker shows where they are furthest from the cubic, measured both ways by dense sampling.

kurbo: equal steps in t, each piece the mid-point quadratic.
fonttools: one smooth (G1) quadratic spline; the joins don't kink, at the cost of more pieces.
colomitchi-half: halving until each piece's mid-point quadratic is within the tolerance.
colomitchi-adaptive and colomitchi-symmetric: splitting where the error just reaches the tolerance, from the start, or from both ends at once.
